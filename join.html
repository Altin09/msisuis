Join MSIS

Every Muslim student at UiS has a place here

Membership is free and open to current Muslim students at the University of Stavanger as well as non-students who are interested in our activities. Join to receive event updates, vote at the General Assembly, and shape the future of MSIS.

Membership benefits

Your voice matters

Muslims are the fundamental building blocks of MSIS. By becoming a member, you help shape our activities and ensure that our community reflects your needs and aspirations, as well as strengthening the Muslim community in Stavanger. Members can vote at the General Assembly and join the board to contribute directly to our initiatives.

  • Cost None - MSIS membership is completely free.
  • Eligibility Muslim students enrolled at UiS.
  • Involvement Get involved in shaping our activities. Receive notifications about upcoming events. Vote at the General Assembly and decide the future direction of MSIS.

MSIS fosters unity, learning, and dialogue among Muslim students at the University of Stavanger.
