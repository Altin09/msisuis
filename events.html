Signature MSIS programmes

These core events can be adapted for future semesters. You may use them as inspiration when planning collaborations.

Dialogue

Dialogsmøte – Bridges of Understanding

March UiS Library Auditorium

An interfaith evening featuring representatives from MSIS, UiS Chaplaincy, and local student groups. Includes keynote reflections, open Q&A, and guided conversation tables.

Why it matters: Builds trust across communities and showcases how faith enriches academic life.

Community

International Dinner & Culture Night

April SiS Student Centre

Students share family recipes, cultural performances, and stories behind their dishes. The night ends with a collective reflection on hospitality, gratitude, and community care.

Logistics tip: Partner with StOr for kitchen access and apply for SiS funding for ingredients.

Spirituality

Friday Prayer & Gathering

Weekly Prayer Room Ø-110

A welcoming khutbah followed by prayer, reflection, and informal catch-ups. Volunteers assist new students with practical guidance and language support.

Accessibility: Provide clear signage to the space and offer quiet time for those who wish to reflect after prayer.

Education

Study Skills & Wellbeing Workshop

Semester start Arne Rettedahls Hus

A practical session co-run with SiS advisors highlighting time management, exam preparation, and maintaining spiritual balance during busy study periods.

Outcome: Attendees receive templates and accountability buddies for the semester.
