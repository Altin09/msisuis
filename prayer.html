Helpful tips

Make the most of the timetable

By having the time tables available, we hope to facilitate your daily worship and the opportunity to host a jamma'ah on campus. Remember to call Adhan, and gather fellow students for congregational prayers whenever possible.

  • Location: Stavanger, Norway
  • Method: University of Stavanger coordinates, Hanafi
  • Angles: 18° Fajr / 17° Isha with DST applied automatically

On-campus reminders

  • Friday prayer: Khutbah start times are announced weekly on Instagram @msisuis, so check the latest update before heading over. We also announce daily prayer times on Instagram, check the Story posted every morning.
  • Prayer spaces: Permanent prayer rooms are in Kjølv Egelands hus and the SiS Student Centre (room Ø-110). Follow posted signage for access.
  • Wudu: Please perform wudu before arriving; ablution facilities are not available inside these spaces.
  • Need support? Reach out through the contact form if you need campus guidance or extra reminders.
