Who we are

MSIS brings together Muslim students to create a space that champions understanding, leadership, and service. Every voice matters in our community.

Community

Welcoming every Muslim student and non-student in Stavanger

We celebrate the breadth of backgrounds represented on campus. Whether you just joined UiS or call Stavanger home, MSIS offers belonging, peer support, and lasting friendships.

Knowledge

Learning rooted in shared values

Study circles, guest lectures, and dialogue sessions explore Islamic perspectives while encouraging critical reflection and academic curiosity.

Collaboration

Partnering with UiS, SiS, and StOr

By working closely with the university and other student organizations, MSIS helps ensure Muslim voices inform campus decisions and inter-organizational projects.

250+ Members, alumni, and friends connected since 2014

  • Workshops and volunteering Develop leadership skills while supporting campus-wide initiatives.
  • Mentorship opportunities Connect new students with experienced volunteers and alumni mentors.

Dialogsmøte with UiS Chaplaincy

Community

International Dinner and Iftar evenings

3 Sep and during Ramadan SiS Student Centre

Students prepared dishes from their home countries, creating a night that blended food, storytelling, and cross-cultural friendships. During Ramadan, we hosted iftar evenings with served meals and reflections, shining a light on gratitude and community care.
